**AptiveAI Agents** is where you work with your agents: you start
[tasks](/concepts/tasks), follow them, answer your agents and set everything
up. This page walks through it from the sidebar down, so you know where each
thing lives. Every section links to the page that explains it in full.

## The sidebar

The sidebar is the same on every page. From the top:

| Row | What it does |
|---|---|
| **New task** | The first page of AptiveAI Agents, where work starts. See [Starting work with an agent](#starting-work-with-an-agent). The control at the end of the row opens a new task *beside* the page you are on, so you can start one without leaving it. |
| **Pinned agents** | Agents you have pinned get a row of their own, right under New task, marked with a pin. Clicking one starts a task with that agent. Each has its own *beside the page* control, which opens a new task with that agent already chosen, next to the page you are on. |
| **Inbox** | What your agents need you to see — see the [Inbox](/concepts/inbox). |
| **Automations** | Every [automation](/concepts/automations) you can see — the tasks that run on their own. |
| **Work** | [A page of cards](#the-work-page) for tasks, automations and the Inbox. |
| **AGENTS** | A short list of agents with their latest tasks under each. See [Agents in the sidebar](#agents-in-the-sidebar). |
| **RECENT** | Every task you can see, newest and most urgent first. See [Recent tasks](#recent-tasks). |
| **Configure & help** | At the very bottom, below a line. [One page of cards](#configure--help) for your agents, connectors and skills, your settings, and help. |

### Recent tasks

**RECENT** lists every task you can see — the ones you started, the ones
shared with you, and the tasks of automations shared with you ([who sees a
task](/concepts/tasks#sharing-and-who-sees-a-task)). It is ordered by what
needs you:

1. Pinned tasks, for everyone in the workspace.
2. Tasks waiting on you — marked with an amber **Needs you** badge.
3. Tasks running now — with a spinner at the end of the row.
4. Everything else, newest first.

The task you are looking at is highlighted. A finished task shows its name and
how long ago it ran; the first fifty are loaded, and **N more** at the bottom
loads more in place. For something older, use the full
[Tasks list](/concepts/tasks#the-tasks-list) under **Work**.

The buttons in the section's header:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Search (magnifier) | Filters the tasks already loaded by name, agent, result, question or status. |
| **Filter tasks** (funnel) | Narrows the list by **Status** (**Needs you**, **Running**, **Finished**, **Failed**), **Started** (**Today** or the last 7 or 30 days), **Agent**, and **Source** (**All**, **By me**, **Automations**, **Shared with me**). Each filter you set shows as a chip under the header with a **Clear** link, and the funnel carries a dot, so a filtered list never passes for the whole one. |
| Density | **Compact** (one line per task), **Normal** or **Detailed**, which adds when the task started. |
| **+** | Opens New task. |

Each task row has a **…** menu — appearing when you point at the row, always
shown on a touch screen. It holds the task's actions: pin, rename, open side
by side, share, stop and delete. They are covered on the
[Tasks page](/concepts/tasks#the-task-menu).

Tasks that an [automation](/concepts/automations) started stack into one
**deck** per automation, with a small count on it. Click the deck to open it in
place and see its tasks one by one; a deck holding a single loaded task opens
the automation instead.

### Agents in the sidebar

**AGENTS** shows a short list, not every agent:

- your **pinned agents**, first, in the order you pinned them (they also keep
  their row under New task);
- then up to three more agents, the ones you used most recently, and then
  others alphabetically;
- then **All agents**, which opens the [Agents page](/concepts/agents#where-agents-appear).

Click the **AGENTS** heading to fold the section or open it. It starts open if
you have a pinned agent and folded if you have none, and it remembers how you
left it in this browser.

Click an agent's name to show or hide what is under it: its latest three
tasks, then **N more** when it has others — each click adds five more in
place — and last, **+ New task**, which starts a task with that agent. An agent
with no tasks yet shows only **+ New task**.

Every agent row has a **…** menu:

- **Start new task** — the same as **+ New task** under the agent.
- **New task in split view** — a new task with this agent, beside the page you
  are on.
- **Configure** — opens the agent's builder. Only shown if you may edit the
  agent.
- **Clone** — makes a copy of the agent. Only shown if you may clone it.
- **Share…** — who can see and use the agent.
- **Pin** / **Unpin** — adds the agent to, or takes it off, the top of the
  sidebar. Pins are yours alone; see
  [Pinning and icons](/concepts/agents#pinning-and-icons).
- **Delete** — only shown if you may delete the agent.

On the narrow icon-only sidebar the **AGENTS** and **RECENT** lists are not
drawn; pinned agents keep their icons, and widening the sidebar brings the lists
back.

## Starting work with an agent

**New task** (`/`) asks **What are we working on?** Your agents are tiles
above the composer, the one you used most recently first. Click one, and the
page becomes that agent's own screen: the headline reads *You're working with
…* with the agent's picture over it, its purpose sits under it, and the agent's [conversation
starters](/concepts/agents#conversation-starters) are ready to click. The
breadcrumb reads **New task / *the agent's name***, the composer shows the
agent as a chip, and whatever you send goes to it. On a phone the tiles are two
to a row, with a one-line purpose. Past ten agents a **Filter agents...** box
appears above them.

You can also reach the same screen from a pinned agent's row, from an agent's
**+ New task** in the sidebar, or from **Start a task** on the Agents page.

When no agent is chosen, a short hint band beside the composer explains that
sending as it is gets an answer, and that choosing an agent has the work done
inside your own systems. Close it and it stays closed in this browser; the icon
that opened it brings it back, and the next close is remembered again.

## The Work page

**Work** opens a page of cards, one per place you can go. It holds what your
agents do and when: [Tasks](/concepts/tasks),
[Automations](/concepts/automations) and the [Inbox](/concepts/inbox), plus
**Productivity** for owners and admins.

## Configure & help

**Configure & help** is one page, in three groups of cards. Opening
`/help` takes you to the same page.

| Group | Cards | Shown to |
|---|---|---|
| **Configure** | **Agents**, **Connectors**, **Skills** | Everyone |
| **Help & settings** | **Ask about AptiveAI**, **Docs**, **Settings**, **Personal sign-ins**, **Notifications**, **Usage** | Everyone |
| **Your enterprise** | **Admin**, **Activity**, **Productivity** | Owners and admins; **Activity** needs billing access |

| Card | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Ask about AptiveAI** | Starts a new task that asks what AptiveAI Agents can help you with, in your language. It searches this documentation and answers straight away. |
| **Docs** | Opens this documentation in a new tab. |
| **Settings** | [Settings](/reference/settings): your plan and AI credit, the app's appearance and your language. |
| **Personal sign-ins** | The [Personal sign-ins](/reference/settings/sign-ins) section of Settings. |
| **Notifications** | The [Notifications](/reference/settings/notifications) section of Settings. |
| **Usage** | Your own AI usage, in the portal. Every person sees their own; owners also see their enterprise's. |
| **Admin** | [The admin area](/administration/overview), in the portal. |
| **Activity** | What your enterprise's agents used, in the portal — see [Activity](/administration/activity). |
| **Productivity** | Goals met and work time, in the portal — see [Productivity](/administration/productivity). |

Each card has up to three links:

| Link | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Goes to the page. **Admin**, **Usage**, **Activity** and **Productivity** open in the portal, and **Docs** opens this documentation; those cards are marked with an outside-link icon. |
| **Docs ↗** | Opens the page of this documentation that explains it. |
| **Ask** | Starts a new task with a prepared question — *Explain Tasks: search the docs, summarise it, and link the pages.* — and opens it, so the answer starts arriving straight away. If your workspace cannot start tasks right now, the question is left typed in New task instead. |

**Ask about Work** and **Ask about Configure & help**, in the page header, do
the same for the whole page: the question asks what you can do under that
section. An Ask task has no agent, so it answers from this documentation — no
computer, and no access to your systems.

## Where to find things

| Looking for | Where it is |
|---|---|
| A task you or a colleague started | **RECENT** in the sidebar, or **Work → Tasks** |
| An agent's tasks | Under the agent in **AGENTS**, with **N more** for older ones; or the agent's **Tasks** button on the Agents page |
| An automation | **Automations**, or **Work → Automations** |
| A question waiting on you | **Inbox**, or the **Needs you** rows at the top of **RECENT** |
| Every agent, or a new one | **All agents** at the end of the **AGENTS** list, or **Configure & help → Agents** |
| Your own sign-ins to websites an agent opens | **Configure & help → Personal sign-ins** — the [Personal sign-ins](/reference/settings/sign-ins) section of Settings |
| Which notifications reach you, and how | **Configure & help → Notifications** — the [Notifications](/reference/settings/notifications) section of Settings |
| Your plan, AI credit, theme | **Configure & help → Settings** |
| Your own usage | **Configure & help → Usage** |
| Language and your account | The account menu, under your avatar in the top-right corner |
| Members, invitations and billing | **Configure & help → Admin**, for owners and admins — see [The admin area](/administration/overview) |
| This documentation, or an answer from it | **Configure & help → Docs**, or **Ask about AptiveAI** |

The **Agents page** lists every agent you can see, as tiles or a table, with
each one's last task and next run. **Start a task** opens New task with that
agent; **New agent** opens the builder.

## On a phone

On a phone the sidebar becomes a bar at the bottom of the screen: **Menu**,
**Work**, **New task** in the middle, **Agents** and **Tasks**. **Menu** opens
the full sidebar as a side panel — the same rows, with **AGENTS** and **RECENT**
in it, the same **…** menus, and **Configure & help** last, below a line. This
is where **RECENT** lives on a phone; there is no separate tasks panel. Rows
are taller for a finger, the **…** menus are always visible and open as a
sheet, and there is no density button. **Agents** in the bar opens the Agents
page and **Tasks** the Tasks list. **Inbox** is in **Menu**, and the bell in
the header shows your unread count.
