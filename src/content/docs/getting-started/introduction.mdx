---

**AptiveAI** builds AI workers for businesses. Two products, one integration
layer:

- **AptiveAI Connect** — your existing ChatGPT or Claude, connected to your
  systems through AptiveAI Connectors.
- **AptiveAI Agents** — configured agents on isolated computers with a browser
  and a desktop, working tasks in any field.

Getting started begins with the **AI Assessment** — on-site or as a
questionnaire on [aptiveai.io](https://aptiveai.io) — which maps your processes
to the right product.
