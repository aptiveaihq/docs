The **admin area** is where your company's account is looked after: the people
in it, their roles, the invitations waiting to be accepted, and — for owners —
billing and usage. It lives on the AptiveAI website at `aptiveai.io/admin`,
beside AptiveAI Agents rather than inside it, and it is open to the **owners** and
**admins** of your enterprise.

Your day-to-day work — [tasks](/concepts/tasks), [agents](/concepts/agents),
[schedules](/concepts/schedules), [connectors](/concepts/connectors) — stays in
AptiveAI Agents. The admin area is only about the account.

## Your account: enterprise, organization, workspace

Everything your company has in AptiveAI Agents sits in one **enterprise** — the
customer account, the thing that is subscribed and invoiced. Inside it:

| Level | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Enterprise** | Your company's account. Roles, billing and AI credit belong here |
| **Organization** | A grouping of workspaces inside the enterprise |
| **Workspace** | Where the work lives: tasks, schedules, agents and computers belong to a workspace |

A new enterprise starts with one organization and one workspace, and most
companies never need more. See [Access and sharing](/administration/access) for
what the levels mean for who sees what.

## Who can enter

| Role | Enters the admin area | What they see there |
|---|:---:|---|
| **Owner** | ✓ | **Overview**, **Users**, **Usage** and **Billing** |
| **Admin** | ✓ | **Users** |
| **Member** | — | *You don't have access to this page* — the admin area is for your enterprise's owners and admins |

Every admin page checks the role for itself, so the menu only lists what you
can open. An admin who opens `/admin` is taken straight to **Users**, the one
page that is theirs. Both roles also see **Agents** in the menu, which takes
you back to AptiveAI Agents.

<Aside type="note" title="Why billing is the owner's alone">
  Billing and usage are about money the company has agreed to pay, so they are
  shown to owners only. An admin who opens **Billing** or **Usage** is told
  that it is visible to the enterprise's owner. Everyone can still see the
  account's status and the AI credit left in AptiveAI Agents, under
  [Settings → Plan & credit](/reference/settings#plan--credit).
</Aside>

## Getting there

- **From AptiveAI Agents.** Open **Configure** in the sidebar and choose the
  **Admin** card — *Members, invitations and billing for your enterprise, in
  the portal*. The card is only shown to owners and admins.
- **From the website.** Signed in at `aptiveai.io`, owners and admins have an
  **Admin** button in the header, and an **Admin** entry at the top of the
  menu on a phone.
- **Directly.** Go to `aptiveai.io/admin`. It is the same page for everyone;
  what it shows depends on your role.

To go back, **Agents** in the admin menu opens AptiveAI Agents. On a phone the
admin menu sits in a bar at the bottom of the screen, with **Account** and
**Home** beside it.

## The pages

### Overview

For owners: your enterprise at a glance.

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Your enterprise** | Its name and billing status — *The enterprise you own. Its members and billing are managed from here* |
| **People** | How many **Members** it has and how many **Pending invitations** |
| **Where to start** | **Invite someone** — add a colleague, or change what they can do — and **See billing** |

An owner whose subscription has ended is taken to **Billing** instead, the
one page still open to them.

### Users

Everyone in your enterprise and every invitation you have sent, on two tabs:
**People** and **Invitations**. Invite people, change a role, remove someone.
See [Users and roles](/administration/users).

### Usage

For owners: the AI credit left, what spent it over the last 30 days by agent
and by person, and every movement of credit. See
[Usage and credit](/administration/usage).

### Billing

For owners: the plan, its status, the payment method and every invoice, to
download as a PDF. It is read-only. See
[Usage and credit → Billing](/administration/usage#billing).

## What AptiveAI handles

Some things about an account are set up by the AptiveAI team rather than from
a screen of yours. Ask your AptiveAI contact, or use **Contact us** on any page
that offers it, for:

- creating your enterprise and inviting its first owner;
- adding workspaces or organizations, or limiting a member to some of them;
- your subscription — creating it, changing how you pay, cancelling it;
- adding AI credit;
- connecting a new [connector](/concepts/connectors) to your company's systems;
- choosing the AI model an agent uses;
- deleting an archived agent for good;
- moving a person who already belongs to another enterprise into yours.

AptiveAI's own staff screens — every customer account, account suspensions,
the computers that agents work on — are run by AptiveAI support and are not
part of your admin area.
