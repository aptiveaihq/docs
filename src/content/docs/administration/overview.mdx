The **admin area** is where your company's account is looked after: its
workspaces, the people in them and their roles, the invitations waiting to be
accepted, and — for owners — seats, billing and usage. It lives on the AptiveAI
website at `aptiveai.io/admin`, beside AptiveAI Agents rather than inside it.
**Owners** and **admins** manage the account there; **every person** can open
it to read their own usage.

Your day-to-day work — [tasks](/concepts/tasks), [agents](/concepts/agents),
[automations](/concepts/automations), [connectors](/concepts/connectors) — stays in
AptiveAI Agents. The admin area is only about the account.

## Your account: enterprise, organization, workspace

Everything your company has in AptiveAI Agents sits in one **enterprise** — the
customer account, the thing that is subscribed and invoiced. Inside it:

| Level | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Enterprise** | Your company's account. Roles, billing and AI credit belong here |
| **Organization** | A grouping of workspaces inside the enterprise |
| **Workspace** | Where the work lives: tasks, automations, agents and computers belong to a workspace |

A new enterprise starts with one organization and one workspace, and most
companies never need more. An owner can add workspaces — see
[Workspaces](/administration/workspaces) — and
[Access and sharing](/administration/access) says what the levels mean for who
sees what.

## Who can enter

| Role | Sees in the admin area |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | **Overview**, **Workspaces**, **Users**, **Usage**, **Activity**, **Productivity** and **Billing** |
| **Admin** | **Workspaces** and **Users** — for the workspaces they administer — and **Usage**, which is their own. **Productivity** too, when they administer every workspace |
| **Member** | **Usage**, their own, and nothing else |

Every admin page checks the role for itself, so the menu only lists what you
can open. An admin who opens `/admin` is taken straight to **Users**, and a
member to **Usage**. Everyone also sees **Agents** in the menu, which takes you
back to AptiveAI Agents. Anyone who opens a page that is not theirs is told so:
*The admin area is for your enterprise's owners and admins.*

<Aside type="note" title="Why billing is the owner's alone">
  Billing is about money the company has agreed to pay, so it is shown to the
  owner only. An admin who opens **Billing** is told that it is visible to the
  enterprise's owner. Everyone can still see the account's status, and their own
  allowance, in AptiveAI Agents under
  [Settings → Plan & credit](/reference/settings#plan--credit).
</Aside>

## Getting there

- **From AptiveAI Agents.** Open **Configure & help** at the foot of the sidebar
  and choose the **Admin** card — *Members, invitations and billing for your
  enterprise, in the portal*. The card is only shown to owners and admins.
  Everyone, members included, has a **Usage** card there that opens their own
  usage.
- **From the website.** Signed in at `aptiveai.io`, owners and admins have an
  **Admin** button in the header, and an **Admin** entry at the top of the
  menu on a phone.
- **Directly.** Go to `aptiveai.io/admin`. It is the same page for everyone;
  what it shows depends on your role.

To go back, **Agents** in the admin menu opens AptiveAI Agents. On a phone the
admin menu sits in a bar at the bottom of the screen, with **Account** and
**Home** beside it.

## The pages

### Overview

For owners: your enterprise at a glance. Its name has **Rename enterprise**.

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Your enterprise** | Its name and billing status — *The enterprise you own. Its members and billing are managed from here* |
| **People** | How many **Members** it has and how many **Pending invitations** |
| **Where to start** | **Invite someone** — add a colleague, or change what they can do — and **See billing** |

An owner whose subscription has ended is taken to **Billing** instead, the
one page still open to them.

### Workspaces

For owners and admins: the workspaces, who works in each, and who administers
them. See [Workspaces](/administration/workspaces).

### Users

Everyone in your enterprise and every invitation you have sent, on two tabs:
**People** and **Invitations**. Invite people, change a role or a seat, remove
someone. An admin sees the people of the workspaces they administer. See
[Users and roles](/administration/users).

### Usage

For everyone: your own allowance and what you can do past it. Owners also see
the wallet and what spent it over the last 30 days by agent, with a
**Transactions** card; owners and admins see each person's capacity. See
[Usage and credit](/administration/usage) and
[Transactions](/administration/transactions).

### Activity

For owners: what your agents did over any period and the AI usage behind it —
tasks, goals met, agents, AI models and AI steps. See
[Activity](/administration/activity).

### Productivity

For owners, and for admins of every workspace: goals met, the agents' work time
and the work they handled in your systems. Admins see it without any money. See
[Productivity](/administration/productivity).

### Billing

For owners: the plan and its status, seats and tiers, the card, the wallet,
every invoice, and cancelling — all of it self-serve. See
[Billing and seats](/administration/billing).

## What AptiveAI handles

You manage your own seats, card, wallet and workspaces. A few things about an
account are still set up by the AptiveAI team rather than from a screen of
yours. Ask your AptiveAI contact, or use **Contact us** on any page that offers
it, for:

- creating your enterprise, with its trial, and inviting its first owner;
- a price of your own, if your company has one;
- adding organizations;
- choosing the AI model an agent uses;
- removing a deleted agent immediately, before its 30 days are up;
- moving a person who already belongs to another enterprise into yours.

AptiveAI's own staff screens — every customer account, account suspensions,
the computers that agents work on — are run by AptiveAI support and are not
part of your admin area.
