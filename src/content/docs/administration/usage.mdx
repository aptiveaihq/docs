Your enterprise pays AptiveAI a monthly subscription, and its agents spend
**AI credit** as they work. This page explains where the credit comes from,
what spends it, where to read it, and what happens when it runs out.

| Where | Who | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| AptiveAI Agents → **Settings → Plan & credit** | Everyone | The account's **Status**, the **AI credit left**, and whether agents **Can start new work** |
| Admin → **Usage** | Owners | Credit left, and the last 30 days by agent |
| Admin → **Usage → Transactions** | Owners | Every charge to the credit and every credit added — see [Transactions](/administration/transactions) |
| Admin → **Activity** | Owners | AI usage, tasks, goals met and models over any period — see [Activity](/administration/activity) |
| Admin → **Productivity** | Owners and admins | Goals met, work time and the work agents handled — see [Productivity](/administration/productivity) |
| Admin → **Billing** | Owners | The plan, the payment method and every invoice |

## Paid access

Agents can start new work only while **both** hold:

- the account's status is **Active** or **Trial**, and
- it has **AI credit** left to spend — more than zero.

Without paid access, no new work starts: nobody can start a task, create or
configure an agent, create an automation or grant a connector, and automations
skip their runs. AptiveAI Agents is not shown at all. Everyone who opens it is sent
to one page that says why, with **Contact us** — and, for an owner, **View
billing**:

| Page | What it says |
|---|---|
| **This account is not active yet** | *AptiveAI is still setting up this account. You will be able to start work once your trial or subscription begins.* |
| **No trial credit is available** | *This account is on a trial, but it does not have enough AI credit to run your agents.* |
| **No AI credit is available** | *The subscription is active, but this account does not have enough AI credit or balance to run your agents.* |
| **A payment is overdue** | *The last invoice was not paid by its due date. Agents cannot start new work until it is settled.* |
| **This subscription has ended** | *Agents and task history are no longer available on this account. To reactivate it, get in touch.* |

The admin area stays open to owners and admins, with the same reason in a
banner at the top, so an owner can still read **Billing** and **Usage**. A
task that was already working when the credit ran out finishes — see
[below](#why-a-task-can-finish-on-the-last-of-the-credit).

The account's status is one of **Awaiting first payment**, **Trial**,
**Active**, **Payment overdue** or **Cancelled**.

## AI credit

Every step an agent takes is charged a little credit — usually a fraction of
a cent — depending on the AI model the agent uses and how much it has to read
and write. Credit is spent by tasks,
by tasks an automation starts, by **Try it** in the agent builder, and by the
short title AptiveAI writes for each task.

### Three kinds of credit, spent in order

| Kind | Where it comes from | Expires |
|---|---|---|
| **Promotional** | A credit link from AptiveAI — see [Credit from AptiveAI](#credit-from-aptiveai) | Yes — unused credit expires on the date it was issued with |
| **Plan credit** | Your plan's monthly AI allowance, added when an invoice is paid, once per invoice | No |
| **Wallet** | Credit AptiveAI adds to your account directly | No |

An agent draws on **promotional credit first** (the soonest to expire first),
then **plan credit**, then the **wallet**. The credit left is the sum of all
three, and it is the number paid access reads.

### Why a task can finish on the last of the credit

What a task will cost cannot be known before it runs, so a task only needs
*some* credit to start, not enough for the whole job. A task that is working
when the credit runs out **finishes**; its last steps take the balance below
zero. The **next** task is refused, and an automation's next run is skipped as
**No AI credit** — without piling up work for when credit returns.

No task is ever stopped halfway for money.

### Credit from AptiveAI

AptiveAI can send credit to a person in your enterprise as an emailed link.
The page it opens — **Accept credit** — shows the amount, when unused credit
expires and when the link itself expires.

<Steps>

1. **Sign in with the address it was sent to.** Signed in as anyone else, the
   page says **Wrong account**.

2. **Accept credit.** It is added to the enterprise you are in, as
   promotional credit.

</Steps>

Accepting credit on an account that is **Awaiting first payment** starts its
**Trial**, which lasts as long as the credit does: it ends when the credit runs
out or expires. A link that has expired, been withdrawn or been used says
*This credit link is not valid*. If AptiveAI withdraws credit, whatever is
unspent is removed; what was already spent stays spent.

## Computer time

Each task that needs a browser gets a [computer](/concepts/tasks#the-live-view).
The time every computer is in use is recorded — including the few minutes one
is kept ready after a task, so the next task starts faster. Computer time costs
credit only if your plan sets a per-minute rate for it; otherwise it is
recorded at $0.

## The Usage page

Owners open **Usage** in the admin area.

**AI credit** — *What your agents draw on.*

| Tile | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Remaining** | Everything agents can still spend — the same number paid access reads |
| **Spent** | What was charged in the last 30 days |
| **Promotional** | Unexpired promotional credit, when there is any |
| **Wallet** | The wallet, when there is anything in it |

With nothing left it says *No AI credit is available, so new tasks cannot be
started. Contact us to add credit.*

**Last 30 days** — *Where AI and computer spend went*, **By agent**. Besides
each agent's name you may see *Tasks with no agent*, *(tested in the
builder)*, *(computer kept warm)* and *(instructions edited)*. Spending is
shown by agent only, never by person.

**Transactions** — a card that opens [Transactions](/administration/transactions),
every charge to the AI credit and every credit added, with what has been
**Used this month**.

<Aside type="note" title="Recent activity moved to Transactions">
  The list of every movement of credit — **When**, **What ran**, **Model** and
  **Amount** — used to sit at the foot of this page as **Recent activity**. It
  now has a page of its own: open the **Transactions** card, or go to
  `aptiveai.io/admin/usage/transactions`. What each row means is on
  [Transactions](/administration/transactions).
</Aside>

For how your agents did over any period — tasks, goals met, AI usage by agent
and by model — see [Activity](/administration/activity) and
[Productivity](/administration/productivity).

## Notices

Owners and admins are told, in the [Inbox](/concepts/inbox) and by email:

| Notice | When |
|---|---|
| **AI credit is running low** | The credit falls below the account's warning level, when one is set. At most once a month |
| **The trial is about to end** | The trial ends within seven days |
| **A payment is overdue** | The latest invoice is unpaid |

Each is sent once per condition, however many tasks run in between. Which of
them reach you, and how, is in
[Notification settings](/reference/settings/notifications).

## When the subscription ends or the account is suspended

Running out of credit only stops new work. Two things stop work that is
already under way:

- **The subscription ends.** *Agents and task history are no longer available
  on this account.* Tasks that are working end at their next step, tasks
  waiting on a question are stopped, computers are shut down, and stored
  sign-ins are no longer used. An owner can still open **Billing**.
- **AptiveAI suspends the account.** Everyone sees *This account is
  suspended*, and work stops the same way. If you believe it is a mistake,
  contact AptiveAI. When a suspension is lifted, stored sign-ins work again
  with nothing to redo.

## Billing

**Billing** in the admin area is the owner's. It is read-only: AptiveAI manages
the subscription, so there is no card form and no cancel button — to change
anything, use **Contact us**.

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Plan** | The plan, its status, the **Monthly price**, the **Next billing date** or **Billing start date**, when it **Ends** if it is ending, and when a trial's credit expires. When agents cannot start, the reason, with **Contact us** |
| **Payment method** | The card on file — brand, last four digits and expiry — or *No card on file. This enterprise is invoiced, and each invoice is emailed to its billing contact.* |
| **Invoices** | Every invoice, with its **Date**, **Amount** and **Status** — **Paid**, **Open**, **Draft**, **Uncollectible** or **Void** — and **Download** for the PDF |
