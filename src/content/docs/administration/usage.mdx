Every step an agent takes spends **AI credit**. Where that credit comes from
depends on who asked for the work: a person's own tasks draw on the
**allowance** that comes with their seat, and automations draw on the
enterprise's **wallet**. This page explains what each person and the owner can
read about it, and what happens when it runs out. How seats, the allowance and
the wallet are bought and managed is on [Billing and seats](/administration/billing).

| Where | Who | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| AptiveAI Agents → **Settings → Plan & credit** | Everyone | The account's **Status**, whether you **Can start new work**, and **Your allowance** |
| Admin → **Usage** | Everyone | Your own usage — and, for owners and admins, more (below) |
| **Configure & help → Usage** | Everyone | The same page, from AptiveAI Agents |
| Admin → **Usage → Transactions** | Owners | Every charge and every credit added — see [Transactions](/administration/transactions) |
| Admin → **Activity** | Owners | AI usage, tasks, goals met and models over any period — see [Activity](/administration/activity) |
| Admin → **Productivity** | Owners, and admins of every workspace | Goals met, work time and the work agents handled — see [Productivity](/administration/productivity) |
| Admin → **Billing** | Owners | The plan, seats, card, wallet and invoices — see [Billing and seats](/administration/billing) |

## Paid access

Agents can start new work for a person only while **both** hold:

- the account is in good standing — its status is **Active** or **Trial**, a
  renewal is not unpaid for more than 7 days, and it is not paused or cancelled;
  and
- that person has something to spend: room in their **allowance**, or **extra
  credit** from the wallet where the owner allows it.

Without it, nothing new starts: nobody can start a task, create or configure an
agent, create an automation or grant a connector, and automations skip their
runs. Everyone who opens AptiveAI Agents sees one page that says why — with
**Contact us**, and for an owner **Finish setup**, **Subscribe again** or **View
billing**:

| Page | What it says |
|---|---|
| **This account is not active yet** | *AptiveAI is still setting up this account. You will be able to start work once your trial or subscription begins.* |
| **This account is disabled** | *Your account is disabled — reach out to your owner, ‹name›, to enable it.* The account is paused: the trial ended, or a renewal went unpaid for 7 days. The owner is sent to [finish setup](/administration/billing#subscribing) |
| **No trial credit is available** | *This account is on a trial, but it does not have enough AI credit to run your agents.* |
| **No AI credit is available** | *The subscription is active, but this account does not have enough AI credit or balance to run your agents.* |
| **A payment is overdue** | *The last invoice was not paid by its due date. Agents cannot start new work until it is settled.* |
| **This subscription has ended** | *Agents and task history are no longer available on this account.* If it was cancelled at your request, the account and its data are deleted the day after the paid period ended |

The admin area stays open to owners and admins, with the same reason in a
banner at the top. A task that was already working when access stopped finishes
— see [below](#why-a-task-can-finish-on-the-last-of-the-credit).

When it is only a **person** who cannot start, because their allowance is used
up, nothing else changes for the team. That person sees *You've used this week's
allowance — it refreshes ‹date›*, or the same for these 5 hours, and
**Settings → Plan & credit** reads *Your allowance is used for now*.

## AI credit

Every step an agent takes is charged a little credit — usually a fraction of a
cent — depending on the AI model the agent uses and how much it has to read and
write. Credit is spent by tasks, by **Try it** in the agent builder, and by the
short title AptiveAI writes for each task. Who pays:

| Work | Paid from |
|---|---|
| A person's tasks, building an agent, **Try it** | The person's **allowance**, and — where the owner allows it — the wallet once the allowance is used |
| A task an automation starts | The enterprise's **wallet** only, never anyone's seat |

During a **trial** there are no seats yet: the team shares one pool of AI
credit, and the page says *Your team is on a trial and shares one AI credit*,
with the **Credit left**.

### Why a task can finish on the last of the credit

What a task will cost cannot be known before it runs, so a task only needs
*some* credit to start, not enough for the whole job. A task that is working
when the credit runs out **finishes**. The **next** task is refused, and an
automation's next run is skipped as **No AI credit** — without piling up work
for when credit returns.

No task is ever stopped halfway for money.

### Credit from AptiveAI

During a trial, AptiveAI can send credit to a person in your enterprise as an
emailed link. The page it opens — **Accept credit** — shows the amount, when
unused credit expires and when the link itself expires.

<Steps>

1. **Sign in with the address it was sent to.** Signed in as anyone else, the
   page says **Wrong account**.

2. **Accept credit.** It is added to the enterprise you are in, as
   promotional credit.

</Steps>

Accepting credit on an account that is **Awaiting first payment** starts its
**Trial**, which lasts as long as the credit does. A link that has expired, been
withdrawn or been used says *This credit link is not valid*.

## The Usage page

**Usage** is in the admin area for **every role**, and is the one admin page a
member can open: a member who goes to `/admin` lands on it. It is also a card
in **Configure & help** in AptiveAI Agents. What you see depends on your role.

### Your usage — everyone

**Your usage** — *Your own AI allowance, and whether you can keep working past
it. Shown as percentages.*

| Row | Shows |
|---|---|
| **5-hour allowance** and **Weekly allowance** | How much of each you have used, as a bar and a percentage, and when it **Refreshes**. *Starts with your next task* until you have used any |
| **Can you start new tasks** | **Yes**, or **Not right now** with the reason |
| **Extra credit from the wallet** | Whether you can **Keep working from the wallet after your allowance** (**On** or **Off**); your **Limit per person**, as *‹n›% used* or *No limit per person*; and whether **The wallet has money** |

The allowance is paced so that a whole period's worth cannot go in one day —
the weekly allowance is what is left, spread over the weeks that remain, and
the 5-hour allowance is a part of it. Both refresh on their own. **Refresh
usage** reloads the figures. Below them, **Your activity** counts **Tasks you
started** and **AI steps** over **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days**.

Who decides extra credit: an owner sees *You set this on Billing, under
Wallet.* Everyone else sees *Set by your owner. Ask ‹owner› to change it.*

### The owner's enterprise

Above **Your usage**, an owner sees the enterprise:

**AI credit** — *What your enterprise has for automations and extra credit.
Each person's seat comes with its own AI credit, which is not counted here.*

| Tile | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Remaining** | What the wallet and any promotional credit can still pay for |
| **Spent** | What was charged in the last 30 days |
| **Promotional** | Unexpired promotional credit, when there is any |
| **Wallet** | The wallet, when there is anything in it |

With an empty wallet it says *The wallet is empty, so automations and extra
credit cannot be paid for. Each person can still work on their seat's credit.*,
with **Add money** to **Billing**.

**Last 30 days** — *Where AI spend went*, **By agent**. Besides each agent's
name you may see *Tasks with no agent*, *(tested in the builder)*, *(computer
kept warm)* and *(instructions edited)*. Spending is shown by agent only, never
by person.

**Transactions** — a card that opens [Transactions](/administration/transactions),
every charge and every credit added, with what has been **Used this month**.

### Team capacity

*Each person's AI capacity on their seat, and how often this month they reached
the limit.*

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Person** and **Seat** | Who, and their tier |
| **Capacity** | **Normal**, **Near limit** or **At limit** |
| **At the limit** | *Not this month*, or *‹n› weeks this month* |

It shows a status, never anyone's credit in dollars. A person who is often at
the limit is the one to move to a bigger seat — see
[Billing and seats](/administration/billing#a-persons-seat). Owners see it for
the whole enterprise. **An admin sees it for the people of the workspaces they
administer**, under their own usage, and nothing else about money.

## Notices

Owners and admins are told, in the [Inbox](/concepts/inbox) and by email:

| Notice | When |
|---|---|
| **The trial is about to end** | The trial ends within seven days |
| **A payment is overdue** | The latest invoice is unpaid |

Each is sent once per condition. Which of them reach you, and how, is in
[Notification settings](/reference/settings/notifications).

## When the subscription ends or the account is suspended

Running out of credit only stops new work. Two things stop work that is
already under way:

- **The subscription ends.** *Agents and task history are no longer available
  on this account.* Tasks that are working end at their next step, tasks
  waiting on a question are stopped, computers are shut down, and stored
  sign-ins are no longer used. An owner can still open **Billing**.
- **AptiveAI suspends the account.** Everyone sees *This account is
  suspended*, and work stops the same way. If you believe it is a mistake,
  contact AptiveAI. When a suspension is lifted, stored sign-ins work again
  with nothing to redo.

## Billing

Seats, tiers, the card, the wallet, invoices and cancelling are all the
owner's, on **Billing** — see [Billing and seats](/administration/billing).
