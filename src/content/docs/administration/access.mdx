Two things decide what a person can see and do. Their **role** — owner, admin
or member — decides which *kinds* of action they may take (see
[Users and roles](/administration/users#roles)). **Access** decides which
*things* they see: which workspaces, which agents, which tasks and schedules.
This page is about access.

The short version: everything is private to its creator until it is shared, and
an admin role does not unlock anyone else's private work. How sharing works —
Can use, Can edit, Everyone in a workspace — is on [Sharing](/concepts/sharing).

## Workspaces and how far a role reaches

Your enterprise holds one or more organizations, and each organization one or
more **workspaces**. A workspace is where the work lives — its tasks,
schedules, agents and the computers they work on.

| Who | Reaches |
|---|---|
| Owners and admins | Every organization and workspace in the enterprise |
| A member invited from the admin area | Every workspace in the enterprise |
| A member limited by AptiveAI | Only the organizations and workspaces they were given |

Invitations sent from **Users** give access to the whole enterprise at the role
you choose. If a member should see only some workspaces, or you need another
workspace, ask your AptiveAI contact — workspaces and those limits are set up
by the AptiveAI team.

<Aside type="note">
  Rights over a shared agent come from a role that covers the workspace the
  agent belongs to. Being an admin somewhere in the enterprise is not enough
  on its own.
</Aside>

## What owners and admins can open

Opening something by its link is wider than seeing it in a list. A **list** shows
your own items and what is shared with you; an owner's list is no longer a view
of everyone's work.

| Role | Opens by link | Edits |
|---|---|---|
| Member | Own items and what is shared with them | Own items, and where they have Can edit |
| Admin | The same, plus any agent in their organization | The same, plus any agent in their organization |
| Owner | Anything in their workspaces | The same as an admin |
| AptiveAI | Anything in any enterprise | Any agent |

An owner opens someone else's **task** to look, not to work: it is read-only,
and its live view is **view-only** — the screen is visible, but the mouse and
keyboard reach nothing. To work on it, **Continue in my own task** starts a new
task of their own. An owner can also open a **schedule** and pause, change or
delete it.

An admin who holds a role on one workspace only edits another person's agent
if it was shared with them as Can edit.

See [Sharing](/concepts/sharing) for the full rules, who can use and who can
edit each kind, and what happens to a person's shares and schedules when they
leave.

## Connectors for personal agents

Each [connector](/concepts/connectors) carries an **Available to personal
agents** switch, which owners and admins turn on or off on the Connectors page.
A personal agent — one not shared with Everyone in a workspace — may only be
granted connectors that have it on, and the rule is checked again every time a
task starts, so turning it off takes the connector away from every personal
agent at its next task. The grants are kept, so turning it back on restores
them.

Leave it off for a connector that reaches something sensitive, and only agents
shared with the whole workspace will be able to use it. Connectors are not
shared person to person.
