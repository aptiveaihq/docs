AptiveAI Agents is paid for **per person**. Everyone who hands work to agents
holds a **seat**, each seat has a **tier**, and the owner pays for all of them
on one card, once a month. [Automations](/concepts/automations) are paid
differently, from a prepaid **wallet**. The owner manages all of it — seats,
the card, the wallet, invoices — from **Billing** in the
[admin area](/administration/overview), and nobody needs to contact AptiveAI to
do it.

| Who | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything on this page. **Billing** is the owner's alone |
| **Admin** | Sees which seat each person has on **Users**, and can invite people into seats that are already paid for. Cannot buy seats or open **Billing** |
| **Member** | Works on the allowance of their own seat, and reads it under **Usage** — see [Usage and credit](/administration/usage) |

## Starting out

Anyone can create an AptiveAI account, but a **workspace is by invitation**:
a new account with no invitation sees *This account is not set up yet* until
someone invites it — see [Users and roles](/administration/users#someone-signed-up-without-an-invitation).
AptiveAI sets up your enterprise, with a trial, and invites its first owner.

During the **trial** your team shares one pool of AI credit and nobody needs a
card. When the trial ends — on its date, or when its credit is used up — the
account is **paused** until the owner subscribes. Nothing is charged before
that.

## Subscribing

When the account is paused, the owner is taken to **Set up your subscription**
(`aptiveai.io/setup`) wherever they go, in steps. **Billing** also offers
**Set up billing**, and **Subscribe again** if a subscription has ended.

<Steps>

1. **Seats and tiers.** Everyone in the enterprise holds a seat. Pick each
   person's tier (everyone starts on **Bronze**), mark anyone who should not
   stay for removal, and **Invite people** who are still missing. **Empty
   seats** buys seats now for people you will invite later. The **Monthly
   total** updates as you go, and nothing is charged yet. A subscription needs
   at least one seat.

2. **Payment details.** Enter the card and its billing address. It is charged
   today for the first month — **Subscribe and pay** shows the amount — and on
   the same day each month after.

3. **Wallet.** Optional: add money for automations, decide whether people may
   keep working from the wallet when their allowance runs out, and set up
   auto-recharge. All of it can be changed later in **Billing**. **Finish**
   ends the setup.

</Steps>

The last page says *Your subscription is active and your workspace is open
again*, with a receipt by email and links to the console and to **Billing**.
People marked for removal in step 1 leave the enterprise when the
subscription starts, and their automations are deleted.

<Aside type="note" title="What members see while it is paused">
  Anyone who is not an owner sees *Your account is disabled — reach out to your
  owner, ‹name›, to enable it.* The owner sees *Your account is paused. Finish
  setup to turn it back on.* A task that is already working finishes. A task
  waiting on a question stays waiting and carries on after the owner subscribes.
  Automations do not run while the account is paused.
</Aside>

## Seat tiers

A bigger seat runs more computers at once and does more tasks a day. Each
person can be on a different tier, and every tier is the same price for
everyone on it.

| Tier | Price | Tasks a day | Computers at once |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bronze** | $35 per seat / month | up to 25 | 2 |
| **Silver** | $70 per seat / month | up to 50 | 3 |
| **Gold** | $100 per seat / month | up to 70 | 3 |
| **Platinum** | $150 per seat / month | up to 100 | 4 |

*Tasks a day* is an upper estimate for a typical mix of work on the default
model, not a limit: a long task, or a larger model, uses more of a seat. A
person can also have as many live views open at once as they have computers.
The current
figures are always on the [pricing page](https://aptiveai.io/pricing). If your
company has a price of its own, it is arranged with AptiveAI, and **Billing**
shows what you pay.

Start everyone on Bronze and move people up when they hand agents more work
than their seat holds. [Usage and credit](/administration/usage#team-capacity)
shows who is near their limit.

## What each person gets

Every seat comes with an AI **allowance** that refreshes. It is paced so that
nobody uses a whole period's allowance in a day: there is a **weekly
allowance** and, inside it, a smaller **5-hour allowance**. Both are shown to the
person as percentages, never as money, under **Usage** and in
[Settings → Plan & credit](/reference/settings#plan--credit), each with the
time it **Refreshes**.

When a person has used the 5-hour or the weekly allowance, they cannot start a
new task for now:

- They see *You've used your allowance for these 5 hours — it refreshes ‹time›*
  or *You've used this week's allowance — it refreshes ‹date›*, and Settings
  says *Your allowance is used for now*.
- A task they already have working finishes; the next one is refused.
- They can start again when the allowance refreshes. The message also says
  *Ask ‹owner› to allow extra credit* — see [the wallet](#the-wallet).

A move to a higher tier gives the person the higher allowance straight away.
Allowance does not carry over from one billing period to the next.

## The wallet

The wallet is prepaid money the owner keeps for two things: **automations**,
which are paid from the wallet and never from anyone's seat, and **extra
credit** for people who reach their allowance. **Billing** shows it as
**Balance**.

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Add funds** | Charges the card on file from $10 to $500 and credits the wallet |
| **When someone runs out** | **Let people keep working from the wallet** — past their allowance, a person's work is paid from the wallet instead of waiting. Off, they wait for it to refresh |
| **Limit per person** | **No limit**, or **Set a limit**: the most one person may draw from the wallet each month, in dollars. Shown while extra credit is on |
| **Auto-recharge** | Tops the wallet up from the card when the balance drops below an amount you choose, by an amount you choose |
| **What an automation costs** | A calculator: how often it should run, and whether the job is **Quick**, **Standard** or **Long**, gives *about ‹amount› a month* |

An automation whose wallet is empty cannot run, and is recorded as skipped —
see [Automations](/concepts/automations#skipped-runs). If a recharge payment
fails, auto-recharge turns itself off and the owner is emailed. Signed-in
people can also try the same calculator on the public
[pricing calculator](https://aptiveai.io/pricing/calculator).

## The Billing page

**Billing** is the owner's page in the admin area, with these cards:

| Card | What it shows and does |
|---|---|
| **Plan** | The plan's **Status** badge, the **Monthly price**, the **Next billing date** and the **Billing start date**. A trial shows when its credit expires |
| **Seat tiers** | For each tier, how many seats the subscription holds, how many are held and how many are free, with **Change seats** |
| **Payment method** | The card on file — brand, last four digits and expiry — with **Update card**, or **Add card** if there is none |
| **Invoices** | Every invoice with its **Date**, **Amount** and **Status**, a search box, and **Download** for the PDF |
| **Balance**, **When someone runs out**, **Auto-recharge**, **What an automation costs** | [The wallet](#the-wallet) |
| **Cancel subscription** | [Cancelling](#cancelling-and-subscribing-again) |

The status is **Awaiting first payment**, **Trial**, **Active**, **Payment
overdue** or **Cancelled**.

### Changing seats

**Change seats** (or **Change** on a tier) opens a dialog for that tier. Use
**One more seat** and **One seat fewer**; the **Summary** shows what changes
before you confirm it.

| Change | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Add seats** | Charged **today**, for the days left in the period. The dialog shows **Due today** and the next invoice |
| **Remove seats** | Takes effect **from the next billing date**, with no refund — *The renewal bills fewer seats* |

A confirmed change shows *Seats updated.* You cannot remove a seat someone
holds: *Remove people or invitations first.*

### A person's seat

On **Users**, a person's **Seat** shows their tier, and **Change seat** opens
*Move ‹name› to ‹tier›?* with what it costs.

| Change | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Up** | The difference is charged today, for the rest of the period, and the higher allowance starts now |
| **Down** | From the next billing date. Until then nothing changes, and there is no refund. The row reads *‹tier› from ‹date›* |
| **Back to the current tier** | Cancels a move you scheduled. *Stays on ‹tier›… No charge* |

### Inviting and removing people

An invitation holds a seat of its tier until it is accepted, revoked or
expires, so you can only invite into seats that are free. **Invite someone**
picks a **Seat** for each person. When there is no free seat of that tier, an
owner is offered **Add ‹n› seat and send**, with the cost today and the next
invoice; an admin is told to *ask an owner to add a seat*. Resending an
expired invitation takes a free seat again.

Removing someone from a subscribed enterprise happens at the end of the
billing period: *‹name› keeps access until ‹date›*, then their seat leaves the
subscription, and **their automations are deleted** — the dialog lists them.
Nothing is refunded for the period. Until then the row reads *Leaves ‹date›*
with **Keep** to undo it. During a trial a removal is immediate and their
automations are paused instead.

### A payment that fails

If a renewal is not paid, the status becomes **Payment overdue**. For the next
**7 days** everything keeps working, and the owner sees *Your payment is
overdue. Update your card by ‹date› to keep your account active.* **Update
card and pay** takes the new card and pays what is owed. After 7 days the
account is paused, exactly as when a trial ends, and the owner pays from
**Update your card**. Paying reopens the account within a few minutes.

### Cancelling and subscribing again

**Cancel subscription** asks for confirmation. The subscription stays active
until the end of the period you have paid for, and nothing more is charged.
**On the day after that date, the account and all its data — agents, tasks,
automations, connectors and files — are permanently deleted. Nothing is
kept.**

While a cancellation is scheduled, **Billing** says *Set to cancel on ‹date›*,
replaces **Next billing date** with **Access until**, and offers
**Reactivate**, which keeps everything. Seats cannot be changed meanwhile.

<Aside type="caution" title="Cancelling deletes the account">
  Cancelling is not a pause. Reactivate before the date to keep your data. An
  ended subscription that has not yet been deleted shows *This subscription has
  ended* with **Subscribe again**.
</Aside>
