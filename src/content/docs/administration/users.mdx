Everyone who uses AptiveAI Agents for your company belongs to your
**enterprise** with one of three roles. People join **by invitation**: an owner
or admin invites them from **Users** in the [admin area](/administration/overview),
they accept, and they are in.

**Users** has two tabs:

- **People** — everyone in the enterprise, with their **Name**, **Email**,
  **Role**, **Seat** and **Workspaces**. Search with *Search people…*. An admin
  sees the people of the workspaces they administer.
- **Invitations** — every invitation sent, with who was **Invited** and by whom,
  the **Role**, the **Status** and when it was **Created**.

## Roles

| Role | In short |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything an admin can do, in every workspace, plus roles, workspaces, seats and billing |
| **Admin** | Manages agents, connectors and members in the workspaces the owner gave them |
| **Member** | Starts and follows tasks |

An owner's role covers the whole enterprise; an admin is an admin of particular
[workspaces](/administration/workspaces), and a member everywhere else. What
each one may do:

| Action | Owner | Admin | Member |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Start tasks, steer them, answer questions, rename and pin them | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Create a personal agent, and configure it | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Create automations | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Write skills for themselves | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Keep [personal sign-ins](/reference/settings/sign-ins) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Edit any agent in a workspace they administer | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Share an agent, a skill, a task or an automation with specific people | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ (their own, or where they have Can edit) |
| Turn on **Everyone in ‹workspace›** | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Grant connectors to agents, and choose which are [available to personal agents](/concepts/connectors#available-to-personal-agents) | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Approve or reject an agent's change request | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Invite members into free seats, add people to a workspace, resend and revoke invitations, take people out of a workspace they administer | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Invite an admin; remove someone from the enterprise | ✓ | — | — |
| Change someone's role or seat | ✓ | — | — |
| Create, delete and restore workspaces | ✓ | — | — |
| Buy seats, change the card, use the wallet; see billing and invoices | ✓ | — | — |
| Read their own usage | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

Only the person who started a task deletes it — see
[Tasks](/concepts/tasks#sharing-and-who-sees-a-task). Starting or
configuring work also needs [paid access](/administration/usage#paid-access),
whatever the role.

<Aside type="note">
  Seeing an agent, a task or an automation is not a matter of role alone —
  everything is private to its creator until it is shared. See
  [Access and sharing](/administration/access).
</Aside>

## Inviting people

**Invite someone** on **Users** opens **Invite members**.

<Steps>

1. **Add each person.** Type an email address and pick a **Role** —
   **Admin** or **Member** (an admin can only invite members) — and a **Seat**,
   Bronze unless you choose another. **+ Add more** adds a row; up to 50 people
   at a time. If you manage more than one workspace, a **Workspace they join**
   field asks which.

2. **Or upload a file.** **Upload CSV** reads a spreadsheet export: a column
   headed `email` (or just a list of addresses) and, optionally, a `role`
   column. A blank or unknown role becomes **Member**, and the dialog says
   which rows it skipped or changed and why.

3. **Send.** **Send invites** creates the invitations and emails each person
   a link.

</Steps>

The result is shown row by row:

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sent** | The invitation exists and the email went out |
| **Not emailed** | The invitation exists but the email was not delivered. Copy the link from **Invitations** and send it yourself |
| **Already invited** | This address already has a pending invitation. Resend it from **Invitations** if they need a new link |
| **Has an account** | The address already belongs to another AptiveAI enterprise, so it cannot be invited. Contact AptiveAI support to move it into yours |
| **Not an address** | Not an email address |

An invitation holds a seat of its tier until it is accepted, revoked or expires.
When there is no free seat of that tier, an owner can **Add ‹n› seat and send**
from the dialog — it shows the cost today and the next invoice — and an admin
is told to ask an owner. See [Billing and seats](/administration/billing#inviting-and-removing-people).

**Owner is never offered in an invitation.** An owner is appointed from an
existing person's row — see [Changing a role](#changing-a-role).

### Managing invitations

Every invitation **expires after 7 days**. Its **Status** is **Pending**,
**Accepted**, **Expired** or **Revoked**. On a pending invitation:

- **Copy link** copies the accept link, to send it another way.
- **Resend** emails it again with a fresh link and a new expiry. The old link
  stops working. Resending an expired invitation takes a free seat again.
- **Revoke** stops the link working at once. The row stays in the list as the
  record that they were invited.

An expired or revoked invitation can be **Delete**d from the list. Its link
already stopped working; you can always invite the person again.

### What the invitee sees

The email links to an invitation page: *… invited you to join … as a member*
(or *an admin*). From there they **Sign in to accept** or **Create an account**.

<Aside type="caution" title="Accept before opening AptiveAI Agents">
  The invitation page asks the invitee to use the address the invitation was
  sent to, and to accept **before** opening AptiveAI Agents, so their account
  joins your enterprise. Signed in with a different address, they see **Wrong
  account** and can sign out and use the right one.
</Aside>

**Accept invitation** puts them in your enterprise with the role and seat you
chose, in the workspace you named.
Opening the link again afterwards says *You have joined …*, with **Open
AptiveAI Agents**. An invitation that has
expired, been revoked or been resent shows *This invitation is not valid —
ask the person who invited you to send a new invitation*.

## Changing a role

Owners change roles from the **Role** on a person's row in **People**. The
rules:

- Only an **owner** changes roles. An admin sees the role but cannot change it.
- Nobody changes their **own** role.
- Only an owner can change or remove another **owner**.
- **The last owner cannot be demoted.** *This is the enterprise's last owner —
  make someone else an owner first.*

To make someone an admin of one workspace only, use **Make admin** on that
workspace — see [Workspaces](/administration/workspaces#people-in-a-workspace).
To hand the enterprise over, make the new person an owner first, then change
your own role — or have them change it.

## Changing a seat

A person's **Seat** is their tier — **Bronze**, **Silver**, **Gold** or
**Platinum**. Only an owner changes it, from **Change seat** on the person's
row, which shows what it costs before you confirm. A move up is charged today
and starts at once; a move down starts at the next billing date. See
[Billing and seats](/administration/billing#a-persons-seat).

## Removing someone

Removing someone from their row in **People** asks *Remove … from this
enterprise?*. **An admin cannot** — their row reads *Ask an owner to remove
them*; an admin can take someone out of a workspace they administer, which
leaves them in the enterprise. Once an owner confirms, removal:

- takes them out of the enterprise. On a subscribed enterprise this happens at
  the end of the billing period: they **keep access until then** (*Leaves
  ‹date›*, with **Keep** to undo it), their seat then leaves the subscription,
  nothing is refunded, and **their automations are deleted**. During a trial it
  is immediate;
- removes everything that was shared **with** them — what they shared with
  others stays shared;
- during a trial, **pauses the automations they created**, so they stop
  spending credit — an owner can open one and turn it back on;
- **keeps** everything they created: tasks, agents and skills stay (and, during
  a trial, automations). Owners can still open them by link, and owners and the
  admins of an agent's workspace can still edit their agents.

Their AptiveAI account itself is not deleted. The same rules as for roles
apply: owners remove people, nobody removes themselves, only an owner removes
an owner, and the last owner cannot be removed.

A person who holds no access any more but is still attached to the enterprise
shows **No access** in place of a role, so they can still be found and
removed.

## Someone signed up without an invitation

Anyone can create an AptiveAI account, but only an invitation puts it in an
enterprise. An account with none sees one page, wherever they go after signing
in: **This account is not set up yet** — *If your company already uses
AptiveAI Agents, ask your administrator to invite you.* Its one action is
**Contact us**.

To let them in, invite the address they signed up with. When they accept, the
account joins your enterprise.
