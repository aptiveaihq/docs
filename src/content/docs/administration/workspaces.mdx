A **workspace** is where a team's work lives: its [tasks](/concepts/tasks),
[automations](/concepts/automations), [agents](/concepts/agents),
[connectors](/concepts/connectors), [skills](/concepts/skills) and the
computers they run on. Your enterprise is the account that is subscribed and
invoiced; the workspaces inside it are how you divide the work. A new
enterprise starts with one workspace, and most companies never need more.

There is **no workspace switcher**. A person who belongs to several workspaces
sees the work of all of them in one list, and the pages that matter say which
workspace each thing is in — see [Working in more than one workspace](#working-in-more-than-one-workspace).

## Who runs a workspace

| Role | In a workspace |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Runs every workspace in the enterprise: creates, renames, deletes and restores them, chooses who administers each, adds and removes people, and sets anyone's main workspace. Also renames the enterprise, and is the only one who sees billing |
| **Admin** | Runs **the workspaces the owner gave them**, and only those: invites people into seats that are already paid for, adds people who are already in the enterprise, removes people from the workspace, renames it, turns on **Everyone in ‹workspace›**, and edits or deletes anyone's agents, connectors, automations and skills in it |
| **Member** | Works in the workspaces they belong to. May belong to several |

An admin of one workspace is an admin there and a member in the others. An
admin cannot create or delete workspaces, make anyone an admin, change a role
in the enterprise, remove anyone from the enterprise, or open **Billing**.
**Tasks stay private** whatever the role: an admin sees someone else's task
only when it was shared with them — see [Access and sharing](/administration/access).

## The Workspaces page

**Workspaces** is in the admin menu for owners and admins. An owner sees every
workspace; an admin sees the ones they administer. Anyone else is told that
only the owner and the admins of a workspace manage workspaces.

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Workspace** | The name. *Your main* marks your own main workspace |
| **People** | How many people belong to it |
| **Admins** | Who administers it, or *No admins yet* |
| **Created** | When it was created |

A workspace does not need an admin: the owner manages it until there is one.

| Action | Who | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **New workspace** | Owner | Asks for a **Workspace name** (*Operations*) and creates it. Names must be different |
| **Rename** | Owner, and the admins of that workspace | Changes the name |
| **Delete** | Owner | See [Deleting and restoring](#deleting-and-restoring-a-workspace) |
| **Show → Deleted** | Owner | Lists deleted workspaces, each with **Restore** |

Select a workspace to open it.

## People in a workspace

A workspace's page lists its **People** — *who works in this workspace, and
their role in it* — with each person's **Role here** and **Seat**, and a
**Main** badge where it is their main workspace. Removing someone here keeps
them in the enterprise, with their seat and their other workspaces.

| Action | Who | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Add person** | Owner, workspace admin | Picks someone already in your enterprise who is not in this workspace yet. They use the seat they already have |
| **Invite** | Owner, workspace admin | The usual invitation, with a **Seat**, into this workspace. It needs a free seat: an admin is told to ask an owner to add one |
| **Make admin** / **Make member** | Owner | Makes them an admin of this workspace, or a plain member again |
| **Make main** | Owner, workspace admin | Sets this as the person's main workspace |
| **Remove from workspace** | Owner, workspace admin | Takes them out of this workspace only |

*Make admin* explains what it gives: the person can invite people into free
seats, add and remove people here, rename the workspace, and edit or delete
anything in it.

On **Users**, each person's **Workspaces** column lists the workspaces they
belong to, with their main one marked *(main)*, and an owner can change the main
workspace there. **Invite someone** asks for the **Workspace they join** when
you manage more than one. An admin sees only the people of the workspaces they
administer, and can invite **Members** only.

## Main workspace

Every person has a **main workspace**: where what they create lands when they
are not asked. It is the one the owner or a workspace admin chose; when none was
chosen, or it was deleted or the person left it, it is the oldest workspace they
belong to.

## Working in more than one workspace

A person in a single workspace sees no workspace controls at all. A person in
several gets, for [tasks](/concepts/tasks), [automations](/concepts/automations),
[connectors](/concepts/connectors) and [skills](/concepts/skills):

- a **Workspace** label on each item, and a **Workspace** filter on the list;
- a **Workspace** field when they create an agent, an automation, a connector
  or a skill, set to their main workspace unless they pick another. They can
  only pick a workspace they belong to.

A task started with an agent lands in the agent's workspace when you belong to
it, and otherwise in your main workspace. A task started without an agent lands
in your main workspace.

## Deleting and restoring a workspace

Only the owner deletes a workspace. **Delete** asks *Delete this workspace?* and
spells out what happens:

- everything in it — agents, connectors, automations, skills and tasks — is
  **hidden from everyone**, and its automations are **paused**;
- **nothing is erased**, and nobody loses their seat;
- the owner can restore it from **Workspaces → Deleted → Restore**. Everything
  comes back, except that its automations stay paused until someone turns them
  on again.

<Aside type="note">
  The enterprise's last workspace cannot be deleted, so there is always
  somewhere to work.
</Aside>

## A person in no workspace

A person who is in the enterprise but in no workspace — never added, removed
from the last one, or whose only workspace was deleted — sees *You're not in a
workspace yet. Ask your owner, ‹name›, to add you to a workspace. Your account
and your seat are kept.* Until someone adds them, they can still open their own
**Usage** and their account. Add them from the **Workspaces** page, on a
workspace's **Add person**.

## Renaming the enterprise

The owner's **Overview** has a card with the enterprise's name and **Rename
enterprise**. Only the owner can change it.
