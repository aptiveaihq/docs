A [standalone agent](/concepts/standalone-agents) is paid from your
enterprise's **wallet**, never from anyone's seat. Everyone who uses it works
on the wallet, whether they hold a seat or not. This page is for the
enterprise **owner**, who manages the wallet, the card and what is charged.

AptiveAI agrees the price of each standalone agent with your company when the
agent is set up, so you know before the first task what it will cost.

## What is charged

| Charge | When |
|---|---|
| **Each completed task** | Once, when a task completes. A task that does not complete, such as one stopped before it finished, is not charged |
| **Each record saved with a result** | Once for each record the agent saves with the result the agreement names. This is for agents paid by what they produce rather than by task |
| **Record storage** | The first 100 GB of an agent's record files is included. Each GB-month over that is $0.10, charged daily |
| **Monthly base** | An optional fixed amount, charged to the wallet once each month (UTC) while the agent is standalone |

For each agent, the first or second line applies, as agreed with you. An agent
can instead be priced **By usage**, charged for what each task used. A setup
fee, when there is one, is invoiced outside the app and is not in the wallet.

Before a task starts, the wallet must be able to cover it. A task already
working when the wallet runs short finishes; only a new task is refused. See
[When a task does not start](/concepts/standalone-agents#when-a-task-does-not-start).

## The wallet

**Billing**, in the [admin area](/administration/overview), shows the wallet to
the owner.

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Balance** | What the wallet holds now |
| **Add funds** | Charges the card on file and credits the wallet |
| **Auto-recharge** | Tops the wallet up from the card when the balance drops below an amount you choose, by an amount you choose |
| **Payment method** | The card on file. **Add card**, or **Update card** |

<Steps>

1. **Add a card.** On **Billing**, **Payment method** → **Add card**.

2. **Add funds.** **Balance** → **Add funds**, then the amount.

3. **Turn on auto-recharge.** Under **Auto-recharge**, set *Recharge when the
   balance drops below, in dollars* and *Amount to add*, then **Save
   auto-recharge**. It needs a card.

</Steps>

If a recharge payment fails, auto-recharge turns itself off and the owner is
told. The same wallet, card and top-up are described, for seat-based
enterprises, on [Billing and seats](/administration/billing#the-wallet).

### An owner with no seats

An enterprise can have standalone agents and no seats: nobody holds a seat and
there is no subscription. This is normal, and it is called wallet-only here.
Everyone in it works on standalone agents only.

On **Billing**, such an owner sees **Payment method**, the wallet with **Add
funds** and **Auto-recharge**, and **Standalone agents** (below). They do not
see seats, invoices or the other seat-based cards, because there are none.

Someone with no seat does not use the full AptiveAI Agents app: its pages take
them to their standalone agent, or to **Your agents** when there are several.
They can still open their account in the portal and manage the wallet.

## The low-balance notice

When the wallet is running low, the owners are told in the [Inbox](/concepts/inbox)
of the standalone app and by email: *The wallet is running low. The wallet that
pays for your standalone agents has ‹amount› left. When it can't cover a task,
new tasks won't start. Add funds or turn on auto-reload.*

It comes when the balance is below $25, or below fifty tasks at the highest
per-task price among your agents when that is more. It is sent once for each
time the wallet is refilled, and not at all when auto-recharge is on.

## Unpaid charges

If the wallet cannot cover a charge, it is paid down as far as it goes and the
rest is **owed**. The wallet then collects what is owed first, from the next
money that arrives, whether you add funds, a recharge goes through or a refund
lands.

While anything is owed, new tasks of a standalone agent do not start, and the
person sees *Not started: the account has unpaid charges for work already done.*
Add funds to clear it. **Unpaid charges** on the Standalone agents card shows
the amount while there is one.

## Refunds

When a charge was a mistake, AptiveAI can refund it. A refund is credited to the
wallet and appears in the transactions list as **Refund of a task charge**, and
on the card as **Refunds**. If you think a task was charged wrongly, contact
AptiveAI.

## Where to read what was charged

| Where | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Billing → Standalone agents**, and the same card on **Activity → Overview** | This month so far (UTC): for each agent its **Pricing**, **Completed tasks**, **Work**, **Storage** and **Charged**, and the total **Charged this month**, with **Refunds**, **Monthly base** and **Unpaid charges** when there are any. Storage shows *‹used› GB of 100 GB included* |
| **Usage → Transactions** | Every charge and credit, one row each. See [Transactions](/administration/transactions) |
| **Activity** | Tasks and usage over time. See [Activity](/administration/activity) |

On **Transactions**, a standalone agent's rows are named:

| Row | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Task charge** | One completed task, at the agent's price |
| **Record charge** | One record saved with a result |
| **Record storage** | The day's storage over the included 100 GB |
| **Monthly base** | The agent's monthly base |
| **Unpaid charges collected** | Money taken from the wallet to pay what was owed |
| **Refund of a task charge** | A refund, credited back |

<Aside type="note" title="The owner sees what was charged, not what it cost to run">
  The pages above show what your wallet was charged. They do not show the
  AI usage or compute behind a task, because the price you agreed does not
  depend on it.
</Aside>
