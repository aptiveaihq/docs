**Productivity** answers one question: is what your agents do worth it? It
shows how many tasks met their goal, how long the agents actually worked, the
work they handled in your systems, and — for owners — what each goal met cost.
Everything on it is measured; nothing is a "time saved" guess.

It is a page of the [admin area](/administration/overview) at
`aptiveai.io/admin/productivity`, the **Productivity** row in the admin menu.
In AptiveAI Agents, owners and admins also find a **Productivity** card on the
**Work** page.

| Role | Sees Productivity |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | ✓ — your own enterprise, with AI usage in dollars |
| **Admin** | ✓ — your own enterprise, **without any money**: no spend tile, no **Is it worth it?** chart, no AI usage columns |
| **Member** | — *Productivity is visible to your enterprise's owners and admins.* |

The page uses the same [time range](/administration/activity#the-time-range)
as Activity, with **Refresh** in the header. Tasks are counted the same way:
tasks **started** in the period, deleted tasks left out.

## Tiles

| Tile | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Goals met** | Goals met ÷ **all tasks** started in the period. The hint gives *N of M tasks*, what **task status alone says** — the share that simply completed — and how many were **not graded** |
| **Agent work time** | How long the agents actually worked on these tasks, with the average per task. Time spent waiting for a person, a question or an approval adds nothing |
| **Spent per goal met** | *Owners only.* The AI usage of all the period's tasks ÷ goals met, with that AI usage in the hint |
| **Work handled** | **Records read** — distinct records your [connectors](/concepts/connectors) returned to the agents — with connector actions, approved actions and pages visited |

**Goals met** and *task status alone says* sit side by side on purpose. A task
can complete and still not do what was asked; the gap between the two is the
work that looked done and was not. What a goal met is, and when a task is
graded, is explained on [Activity](/administration/activity#what-a-goal-met-is).

A task with no verdict never counts as met, so many ungraded tasks pull the
rate **down** rather than inflate it. A line under the tiles says how many
tasks were not graded, and why that happens: *grading is off, the workspace is
regulated, or the grade has not arrived yet.*

<Aside type="note" title="Two different goal-met rates">
  Productivity divides goals met by **all tasks** in the period. The
  [Models tab](/administration/activity#models) on Activity divides by
  **graded tasks only**, and shows how many were not graded. Each page says
  which it uses.
</Aside>

## Charts

- **Task outcomes** — each period's tasks: **Goal met**, **Partly met**,
  **Blocked**, **Not met**, **Stopped** and **Not graded**.
- **Why tasks didn't meet the goal** — what stood in the way of tasks that
  did not meet their goal (missing information, a missing file, a sign-in, the
  live view, not allowed, a system error, out of scope, something else), and
  how ungraded tasks ended (stopped by a person, an approval declined or timed
  out, AI usage ran out). No task is counted twice.
- **Is it worth it?** — *Owners only.* AI usage per period; point at a bar for
  what each goal met cost, with a line of the cost per goal met over the
  periods that had one.

## By agent

One row per agent: **Tasks**, **Goals met**, **Success** (goals met ÷ tasks), **Work time**, work time **Per task**, **Records read**, **Connector
actions** and **Approved actions**. Owners also see **AI usage** and AI usage
**Per goal met**.
