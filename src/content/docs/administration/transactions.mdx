**Transactions** is your enterprise's ledger: every charge to its
[AI credit](/administration/usage#ai-credit) and every credit added, newest
first. The amounts are what was actually charged or added — nothing on this
page is an estimate worked out later. It is at
`aptiveai.io/admin/usage/transactions`, and it is for **owners**: anyone else
is told *Transactions are visible to your enterprise's owner.*

## The Transactions card

[Usage](/administration/usage#the-usage-page) carries a **Transactions** card —
*Every charge to your AI credit and every credit added, newest first.* The
whole card is a link to this page. On a wider screen it also shows
**Used this month**: what has been charged since the first of the month, with a
small line of each day's charges. Only charges count there — credit added, a
monthly allowance arriving or promotional credit expiring never does. While
the figure loads, or if it cannot be read, the card shows *Your ledger*
instead.

## The ledger

The page holds one table, **Recent activity** — *Newest first* — 25 rows to a
page.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **When** | When the charge or the credit happened |
| **What ran** | The agent, by the name it had at the time; *A task* when there was no agent; or, for credit, what kind of credit it was |
| **Model** | The AI model the agent used, by name — *A model* when it has no display name, and a dash when no model was involved |
| **Amount** | **Negative** for a charge, positive for credit arriving |

A row with no agent is named by its kind:

| Row | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Period reset** | Your plan's monthly allowance, added when an invoice was paid |
| **Promotional credit** | A credit link from AptiveAI was accepted |
| **Promotional credit expired** | Unused promotional credit reached its expiry |
| **Promotional credit revoked** | AptiveAI withdrew unspent promotional credit |
| **Wallet overflow** | Spending that went on to the wallet once the plan credit was used |
| **Manual adjustment** | AptiveAI changed the balance by hand |

Amounts smaller than a cent are shown in full, such as `-$0.004396`, because
rounding a step to `$0.00` would read as a broken meter.

<Aside type="note" title="One step can be more than one row">
  Credit is spent in order — promotional first, then plan credit, then the
  wallet. A charge that empties one kind and continues into the next leaves a
  row for each, with its own part of the amount. Add the rows together for the
  whole charge.
</Aside>

<Aside type="note" title="Rows of $0.00 are on purpose">
  Every step an agent takes leaves a row, even when nothing was charged — for
  example a step with no credit left to draw on. A $0.00 row is the record that
  the step happened, not a broken meter. AptiveAI's own background checks on
  your tasks are recorded at $0.00 too: you are never charged for them.
</Aside>

The ledger names what ran, not who started it: it has no column for people.
Where your AI usage went, agent by agent, is on
[Usage](/administration/usage#the-usage-page) and
[Activity](/administration/activity).
