**Settings** holds your own preferences for AptiveAI Agents — open it from
**Configure & help** at the foot of the sidebar. Everything on it is **per person**: your sign-ins,
your theme, the notifications you receive. The page is organised into the
sections below, and the side navigation jumps between them.

## Plan & credit

Whether this account can start new work, and how much of your allowance is left.

| Row | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Status** | **Awaiting first payment**, **Trial**, **Active**, **Payment overdue** or **Cancelled** |
| **AI credit left** | The credit the account can still spend on AI |
| **Agents** | **Can start new work**, or the reason it cannot — such as *This account is disabled* or *Your allowance is used for now* — with **Contact us** |
| **Your allowance** | Your **5-hour allowance** and **Weekly allowance** as bars, each with when it **Refreshes** (or *Starts with your next task*), and *Approaching limit* or *Limit reached* when it is close. *You have no allowance for this billing period* when your seat has none |

Agents can start new work only while the account is in good standing *and* you
have room in your allowance — see [paid access](/concepts/agents#paid-access).
Under the allowance, **See your usage** opens your **Usage** page: your
allowance, extra credit from the wallet and what you have run. An enterprise's
owner also sees links to **Billing**, **Usage** and **Activity** in Admin, where
the plan, seats, invoices and credit are. How the allowance works is on
[Usage and credit](/administration/usage#the-usage-page).

## Personal sign-ins

Accounts your agents can sign in to while they work for you — your login to a
supplier portal, a carrier's site, a web app your team uses. The password is
stored encrypted and is never shown again, not to you and not to the agent.

See [Personal sign-ins](/reference/settings/sign-ins) for how they are used.

## Appearance

Customize the look and feel of AptiveAI Agents: pick one of its sixteen colour
themes, or **System** to follow your device's light or dark setting.

## Notifications

Control which notifications you receive, and how: in the app, by email, and as
desktop notifications on this device.

See [Notification settings](/reference/settings/notifications) for each
control.

## About

Application information: the running **Version** — click it to copy — handy
when you report a problem, so we know exactly which build you are on.

## Elsewhere

A few things you might look for here live elsewhere:

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| **Language** — English or Spanish | The account menu, under your avatar. Your choice follows you to any device you sign in on |
| **Your account** | **Account** in the account menu |
| **Your usage** — your allowance and activity | **Usage** in **Configure & help**; every person has it |
| **Your team** — members, invitations, roles, workspaces and billing | **Admin**, for owners and admins |
| An agent's own sign-ins | The agent's builder, under **Configure → Sign-ins** |

<Aside type="note">
  **Configure & help**, at the foot of the sidebar, gathers Settings, personal
  sign-ins, notifications, Usage and Admin in one place — with a link to the docs
  for each one — beside a way to ask about AptiveAI and a link to these docs.
</Aside>
