**Settings → Notifications** decides which of the [Inbox's](/concepts/inbox)
notifications reach you, and where. It has three cards. All are yours alone —
nobody else's notifications change.

## Background alerts

A desktop notification when something needs you while the AptiveAI Agents tab is in
the background.

- **Desktop notifications** — turns them on or off. This setting is **per
  device**: turn it on in each browser where you want it.
- **Send test notification** — checks that your browser actually shows one.

If your browser has blocked notifications for AptiveAI Agents, the card says so
and how to allow them. Browsers that cannot show them — iOS among them — get an
explanation instead of the switch.

## Text messages

AptiveAI can text you about your tasks and your account. It is **optional** —
you never need it to use AptiveAI — and it is **off until you agree**.

1. Enter your **Mobile number** (US mobile numbers only).
2. Turn on **Agree to texts**. The switch starts off, and it reads: *Text me
   about my AptiveAI tasks and account. Message frequency varies. Msg & data
   rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.* Links to the Terms
   and the Privacy Policy sit beneath it.
3. Press **Save**. The card then shows *Texts are on for ‹your number›* and
   the date you agreed. **Change number** asks you to agree again for the new
   number; **Turn off texts** switches them off, and you can opt in again at any
   time.

Texts come from AptiveAI's one toll-free number, +1 844-571-9591, the same
number that businesses' agents use. A notice is short, starts "AptiveAI:", links
to the console and carries no task names. The first text the number ever sends
you ends " Reply STOP to opt out."

<Aside type="caution" title="Texts are not available yet">
  Text messages start by themselves once the mobile carriers approve
  AptiveAI's number, expected mid-November 2026. Until then you can opt in, but the **Text** setting of each
  notice cannot be turned on yet, and the card says *Texts start once
  AptiveAI's number is approved by the carriers.* Nothing is texted to you
  before that.
</Aside>

**Reply STOP to stop all texts from the number.** That covers AptiveAI's notices
and every business's agents that text you through AptiveAI Messaging. The card
then says you replied STOP on that date and that texts are off, and **you cannot
turn them back on here**. To start again, write to
[dev@aptiveai.io](mailto:dev@aptiveai.io). Reply **HELP** to any text to get
that contact.

## What you are told about

One row per kind of notification, each with three settings, **On** or **Off**:

| Setting | Where it reaches you |
|---|---|
| **In the app** | The bell, the Inbox page and — when turned on above — desktop notifications |
| **Email** | Your account's email address |
| **Text** | Your mobile number, from AptiveAI's number. Off for every kind to begin with, and it cannot be changed until you have agreed to texts in the card above — the list says *Texts need your opt-in under Text messages above.* |

The kinds, and what triggers each, are listed on the
[Inbox](/concepts/inbox#what-you-are-told-about) page. Out of the box everything
is on in the app, and email is on only for messages and shares addressed to you
and for the account's billing notices.

**A task is waiting for your answer or approval** cannot be turned off in the app — it is
*always shown*, because a task that has stopped to ask you something has no
other way to say so. Its email setting is yours to change.

Every email carries an unsubscribe link that turns this same setting off.
Text notices carry no task or business names; the unsubscribe for texts is
replying STOP.

### Saving

Changes take effect when you press **Save preferences**; until then the card
shows **Unsaved changes**. After saving, the card shows what was stored.

<Aside type="note">
  Emails are sent in English, whatever language AptiveAI Agents is set to.
</Aside>
