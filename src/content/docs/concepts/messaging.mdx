**AptiveAI Messaging** lets your agents send **text messages** and **emails**
to people who do not use AptiveAI Agents: the drivers who missed a step, the
customer whose order changed, the supplier waiting on an answer. It is a
[connector](/concepts/connectors) that AptiveAI runs for you: texts go out from
AptiveAI's own toll-free number, +1 844-571-9591, and emails from an address of
your own on AptiveAI's sending domain. Email is included in your plan, up to 250
emails a day per business; texts are paid from your enterprise wallet. There is
no number to request and nothing to verify:
AptiveAI registers the number with the carriers itself.

It sits beside the connectors you sign in to yourself. If you already send
texts through your own Twilio account, the Twilio SMS connector keeps working
as it does; AptiveAI Messaging is for everyone else, with no account of your
own to set up.

<Aside type="note">
  Only an **owner of your enterprise** can turn AptiveAI Messaging on, change
  its settings, turn it off or remove it, because texts are paid from the
  enterprise's wallet. A workspace owner cannot. Everyone else sees the
  card with *Ask your owner to turn this on*.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="Not for health information">
  AptiveAI Messaging is not offered under a HIPAA Business Associate
  Agreement. Do not use it to send protected health information or other
  health details, in a text or in an email; the
  [Terms of Service](https://aptiveai.io/terms) do not allow it.
</Aside>

## Turning it on

1. Open **Connectors** from the sidebar and choose **AptiveAI Messaging** in
   the catalogue.
2. Press **Turn on**. A dialog says what it does and what it costs: agents you
   allow can text and email people outside AptiveAI Agents, email is included
   in your plan up to 250 a day, texts are paid from your enterprise wallet, a
   task someone is watching asks before it sends unless someone pressed
   **Approve all** in it, and an automation set to run on its own sends without
   asking.
3. Press **Turn on** again. Your enterprise now has one AptiveAI Messaging
   connection, owned by the organization rather than by you, so it keeps
   working if you leave.

**Email works straight away.** Texts start by themselves once the carriers
approve AptiveAI's number (see [How texts are sent](#how-texts-are-sent)): until
then the connector's page says texts are **Not available yet**, agents are not
offered the text tool, and a text send is refused without a charge.

The connection has its own page, **Connectors → AptiveAI Messaging**, with
these sections:

| Section | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Whether email is ready and the address it sends from; whether texts are **Available** or **Not available yet**; what was spent this month |
| **Email** | The **Sender name** — the From name on every email and the start of every text — and the **Reply-to address**, where replies go |
| **Texts** | Whether texts are available, and that they go out from AptiveAI's number starting "AptiveAI for" and your sender name. There is nothing to fill in |
| **Spending** | An optional **Monthly limit** in dollars for texts, and this month's spend against it. Email is included and shows no charge |
| **Activity** | Every message your agents sent |
| **Opt-outs** | Who cannot be messaged, and why |
| **Sharing** | Who may grant AptiveAI Messaging to their agents |
| **Danger zone** | **Turn off** and **Remove** |

## How texts are sent

Every text your agents send goes out from **one AptiveAI toll-free number,
+1 844-571-9591**, which AptiveAI registers with the US carriers. AptiveAI's own
notices to its users go out from the same number. You do not request a number,
fill in a form or wait for a review of your business, and the number costs you
nothing.

- **Every text starts "AptiveAI for ‹your sender name›: "**, so the person
  knows which business it is from. Set the name under **Email → Sender name**.
- **The first text AptiveAI's number ever sends to a phone number ends " Reply
  STOP to opt out."** That counts across all of AptiveAI, not per enterprise: if
  the number has already texted that person for another business or for
  AptiveAI itself, yours does not repeat it.
- **STOP stops everything from the number.** A person who replies STOP to any
  text gets no more texts from AptiveAI's number: not from your agents, not
  from any other business's agents, and not AptiveAI's own notices. **HELP**
  gets a reply naming AptiveAI and its support contact, dev@aptiveai.io. See
  [Opt-outs](#opt-outs) for how this shows up on your list.

## Sharing it and granting it to an agent

Two steps, as with any connector:

1. **Share it** from its **Sharing** section with the people who should use it.
   They get **Can use** only: whatever share someone holds, only an owner of
   your enterprise changes the settings, the limit, turns it off or removes it.
2. **Grant it to an agent** in that agent's builder, like any other connector.

## What an agent can do with it

An agent granted AptiveAI Messaging gets three tools:

| Tool | What it does | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| **Send a text message** | Texts one message to a list of US phone numbers | 1 to 50 numbers; 1 to 1,600 characters |
| **Send an email** | Emails one message to a list of addresses | 1 to 50 addresses; subject up to 200 characters; plain-text body up to 20,000 characters |
| **Check message status** | Looks up whether messages this task sent were delivered | Only messages from the same task |

Sending to a list is one call, so one approval covers the whole batch — "text
every driver on this list" is one decision, not fifty.

### Texts

- **US numbers only.** A number from another country is refused.
- **Every text starts "AptiveAI for ‹your sender name›: "** — "AptiveAI for
  Acme Logistics: …" — so the recipient knows who it is from, as carriers
  expect. The name is written in plain letters, with accents removed.
- **The first text to each number ends with " Reply STOP to opt out."** That is
  once per phone number across all of AptiveAI, not once per enterprise or per
  agent.
- **Curly quotes, long dashes, ellipses and non-breaking spaces are replaced**
  with their plain equivalents, so the message stays in the standard text
  alphabet.
- **A text is billed in parts** (segments): up to 160 characters in one part,
  153 per part when it is longer. A character outside the standard alphabet,
  such as an emoji, makes it 70 characters in one part and 67 per part. The
  approval card and the Activity list always show the number of parts.

### Emails

- They are sent **from your sender name at your enterprise's own address on
  AptiveAI's sending domain** — `Acme Logistics <acme-logistics@notify.aptiveai.io>` —
  with your **Reply-to address**, so replies come to you.
- The body is plain text in a simple layout, with no AptiveAI marketing.
- Every email carries an **unsubscribe link** and a one-click unsubscribe
  header. No attachments.
- **Included in your plan, up to 250 a day.** Each business may email at most
  250 people a day (counted per recipient; the day resets at midnight UTC). A
  send that would pass it is refused before anyone is asked, the agent is told
  to try again tomorrow, and the enterprise's owners get a notification, at most
  once a day.

<Aside type="caution" title="Texts are not private.">
  A text message is not encrypted end to end. The agents are told to keep
  personal details out of messages and to say only what the
  recipient needs to act — "Please call the office about today's change".
  Write your agents' instructions the same way.
</Aside>

## When it asks first

A message cannot be unsent, so a send is a write that asks first, like any
other connector write.

| Where the send happens | What happens |
|---|---|
| A task someone is watching | **Each send asks**, until someone presses **Approve all** in that task. The card shows the recipients (all of them, folded past ten), the message exactly as it will arrive, the number of parts and the charge ("Included" for email). After **Approve all**, later sends in that task go out without a card |
| An **Autonomous** [automation](/concepts/automations) | The agent **sends without asking**, and the transcript records each send |
| An **Interactive** automation | It stops at each send and waits, like a watched task, unless **Approve all** was pressed in that task |
| The **Try it** pane of the agent builder | **Nothing is ever sent or charged.** The agent is told what the send would have done — how many people, how many parts, about what it would cost |
| A skill's trial or a training test | **Nothing is ever sent or charged**, whatever changes you let the test make. The agent is told what the send would have done, as in the **Try it** pane |

## Who an agent may message

An agent can only message a phone number or address that came into the task
from somewhere you trust:

- a message a person wrote in the task;
- what a connector returned in the task, including the files it returned;
- the agent's knowledge files, or a file attached to the task;
- text the agent read on a web page with its browser;
- the output of code the agent ran in the task, or a page of these docs.

A number or address the agent produced from nowhere — made up, or remembered
from another task — is not sent, and the agent is told which recipient was not
found. Each message records where its recipient came from, and the Activity
list shows it.

<Aside type="caution" title="A web page counts as a source.">
  Because page text is a source, a page an agent visits could plant a number.
  What limits the damage is the 50-recipient cap per send, the 250-a-day email
  limit, your monthly limit for texts,
  the opt-out list, the sender name at the start of every text, and the record
  of where each recipient came from. Grant AptiveAI Messaging to an agent an
  Autonomous automation runs only when you are comfortable with that.
</Aside>

A send is also refused, before anything is charged, when:

- a recipient is on your enterprise's opt-out list, or replied STOP to
  AptiveAI's number (to any business);
- a number is not a US number;
- AptiveAI Messaging is turned off, or texts are not available yet;
- an email would pass the business's limit of 250 emails a day;
- a text would pass your monthly limit, or your wallet cannot cover it.

These checks run on every send, also after **Approve all**: it only skips the
question.

If any recipient in a batch is refused, nothing in that batch is sent. The
agent is told which recipients failed and why, and may send again to the rest.

## What it costs

**Email is included in your plan**, up to 250 emails a day per business: no
charge, and no wallet needed. The approval card and Activity show it as
**Included**.

**Texts are paid from your enterprise wallet** — never from the AI credit
included with your plan or from promotional credit. Today a text costs about
**1.8¢ per part**; the turn-on dialog and the
[pricing page](https://aptiveai.io/pricing) always show the current figures. The
toll-free number itself costs you nothing. The up-to-160-character part count
includes the opening "AptiveAI for ‹your sender name›:".

- **A text's charge is known before it goes out**, so a send your wallet
  cannot cover is refused rather than run up as a debt. Add funds to your
  wallet to send again — having AI credit is not enough. See
  [Usage and credit](/administration/usage).
- **A monthly limit** in the **Spending** section stops texts once this
  month's texting would pass it.
- When a send is refused for the wallet, the monthly limit or the daily email
  limit, **the enterprise's owners get a notification**, at most once a day.
- If the carrier refuses a text outright, that recipient's share is refunded. A message that was accepted and then fails to
  arrive is not refunded.

Messaging spend shows up as its own line: on the task, beside AI usage, and on
the wallet card of [Activity](/administration/activity). It is never counted as
AI usage.

## Opt-outs

Two lists keep people from being messaged. Between them, nobody on either can
be messaged by any of your agents.

**Your enterprise's opt-out list** is the one on the **Opt-outs** section.

| How someone gets on it | Shown as |
|---|---|
| They reply **STOP** to a text your agents sent them | **Replied STOP** |
| They replied STOP to AptiveAI's number after a text from some other business or from AptiveAI itself, and your agents had texted them before | **Opted out elsewhere (a STOP to AptiveAI's number, or the carrier's list)** |
| They use the unsubscribe link in an email and confirm | **Unsubscribed** |
| An email to them bounces permanently | **Bounced** |
| They mark an email as spam | **Marked as spam** |
| An owner adds them | **Added by an owner** |

**AptiveAI's own STOP list** covers every business at once. A person who
replies STOP to AptiveAI's number is on it for good, whichever business or
AptiveAI notice they were answering, and no agent of any enterprise can text
them. It is not something an owner edits. Your Opt-outs section shows such a
person only if your agents had texted them; it does not list people you never
messaged. A send to anyone on it is refused before the approval card, and
before any charge.

An owner can add a number or address to your own list under **Opt-outs → Add an
address**. Nobody can take an address off a list. The unsubscribe link in an
email opens a page that asks the recipient to confirm, so a mail scanner that
follows links cannot unsubscribe anyone by accident. Email opt-outs apply to
your enterprise only.

## Activity

The **Activity** section lists every message your agents sent: when, the
channel, the recipient, where the recipient came from, the status, the number
of parts, the charge, and whether a person approved it or an automation sent
it, with a link to the task. Filter it by channel, status or agent.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Queued** | Charged and about to go out |
| **Sent** | Handed to the carrier or the mail service |
| **Delivered** | The carrier or the recipient's mail service confirmed it |
| **Failed** | It was accepted but could not be delivered |
| **Bounced** | The recipient's mail service rejected the email |
| **Marked as spam** | The recipient reported the email |
| **Refused** | Refused outright, and refunded |
| **Unknown** | It is not known whether it went out; it is not sent again on its own |

## Turning it off, and removing it

- **Turn off** pauses it: every send is refused at once. The history and the
  opt-out list are kept, and an owner can turn it on again at any time.
- **Remove** turns it off for good. The messages sent and the opt-out list are
  kept.

AptiveAI may turn AptiveAI Messaging off for an enterprise when it sees abuse —
for example many complaints, opt-outs or bounces. While AptiveAI has it turned
off, only AptiveAI can turn it back on, and it cannot be removed; contact your
AptiveAI contact. Messages may only go to people who agreed to receive them,
never to a purchased list, and carriers' rules apply (see the
[Terms of Service](https://aptiveai.io/terms)).

## Security

What protects the people your agents message, and your enterprise:

| Protection | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Owner control** | Only an owner of your enterprise turns it on, sets the monthly limit, adds to the opt-out list, turns it off or removes it. The connection belongs to the organization, not to a person |
| **A person approves** | On a task someone is watching, each send asks until someone presses **Approve all** in that task; the checks below still run on every send. The **Try it** pane, a skill's trial and a training test never send. See [When it asks first](#when-it-asks-first) |
| **Recipient guard** | An agent can only message a number or address that came into the task from a trusted source, and every message records where its recipient came from. See [Who an agent may message](#who-an-agent-may-message) |
| **Limits** | At most 50 recipients per send, 250 emails a day per business, US numbers only for texts, and your monthly limit for texts. A text your wallet cannot cover is refused before it goes out |
| **Opt-outs** | A STOP reply stops every text from AptiveAI's number to that person, for every business and for AptiveAI's own notices. An unsubscribe, a bounce or a spam report blocks that email address for every agent of your enterprise, and nobody can take an address off a list. The unsubscribe page asks the recipient to confirm, so a mail scanner cannot unsubscribe anyone. A **HELP** reply gets AptiveAI's support contact, dev@aptiveai.io |
| **Sender identity** | Every text comes from AptiveAI's one toll-free number, registered with the US carriers by AptiveAI before texts start, +1 844-571-9591, and starts "AptiveAI for" and your sender name. Email comes from AptiveAI's sending domain, `notify.aptiveai.io`, sent through Amazon SES and kept apart from AptiveAI's own account email (sent through Azure Communication Services), authenticated with SPF, DKIM and DMARC |
| **Signed updates** | Delivery updates and STOP replies from the delivery service are accepted only when the service has signed them |
| **Encryption in transit** | Messages travel encrypted between AptiveAI and the delivery service. A text is not encrypted once it reaches the mobile carrier, so keep messages to what the recipient needs to act on |
| **A record of every message** | **Activity** keeps the recipient, where it came from, the message as sent, whether a person approved it or an automation sent it, its status and its charge. Passwords and codes sent to a task are hidden in the stored copy |
| **Abuse monitoring** | AptiveAI watches complaint, opt-out and bounce rates, and turns AptiveAI Messaging off for an enterprise that abuses it |
| **No health information** | AptiveAI Messaging is not offered under a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement and must not carry health information |

## Who may do what

| Action | Who |
|---|---|
| Turn it on, change the sender name, reply-to address and monthly limit, add to the opt-out list, see Activity, turn it off or remove it | An owner of the enterprise |
| Share it | An owner of the enterprise |
| Grant it to an agent | Anyone it is shared with, in an agent they may edit |
| Have an agent send | Anyone whose agent it is granted to and who may use it |
| Turn it off for abuse, and back on after that | AptiveAI |
