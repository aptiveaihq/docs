Tasks, automations, agents, skills and connectors are shared the same way. Whatever you
create is yours alone until you share it. The chip at the top of each one
shows who has access — **Only you**, **You + 2**, **Everyone in Sales** — and
selecting it opens the share box. In lists, the same chip is a badge, and
**Shared with me** in the Tasks list's **Sharing** filter shows what others
shared with you.

## The share box

- **Add people** from the same workspace. Each person gets a level:
  - **Can use** — they see it and work with it: run the agent, follow the
    automation, grant the skill to an agent they edit.
  - **Can edit** — they can also change it and share it with others.
- **General access** — **Only people added**, or **Everyone in ‹workspace›**.
  Only an owner or admin of that workspace can turn "Everyone" on, and it is
  always **Can use**: a workspace never gets Can edit.
- Changes apply as you make them. **Done** closes the box.
- A skill's box opens from **Who can use** in its row menu or from its editor.
  A connector's opens from **Who can use it** in its panel.

A task has no choice of level: people added can see it and watch it, read-only.
Only the person who started a task can share it, rename it or delete it.

## Who can do what

| | Task | Automation | Agent | Skill | Connector |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The person who made it | Everything | Everything | Everything | Everything | Everything |
| Added, or Can use | See and watch it, read-only | See it and all its tasks, read-only | See it, @mention it, start tasks with it | See it, grant it to an agent they edit | See it, its status and its tools |
| Can edit | — | Change, pause, **Run now**, share; answer and approve in its tasks | Change and share | Change and share | Change its name, settings or sign-in, test it, disconnect it |
| Everyone in ‹workspace› | Same as Can use | Same as Can use | Same as Can use | Same as Can use | Same as Can use |

Only the creator deletes a task or a skill; an automation is deleted by its
creator or by a workspace owner or admin. Knowledge files follow
their agent: whoever can edit the agent edits its files.
Granting a connector to an agent is for owners and admins, and whether a
personal agent may hold a connector is a setting on the connector (see
[Access](/administration/access#connectors-for-personal-agents)).

## What owners and admins can open

Opening by link is wider than listing. Your lists show your own items and what is
shared with you — nothing more, except automations for owners and admins.

- **The owner** can open anything in any workspace by its link, but it does not
  appear in their lists. A task they open is **read-only**.
- **Automations are listed, not just opened**: owners and admins of a workspace
  see every [automation](/concepts/automations) in it in their list, with what
  it costs, and can change, pause, run and delete it.
- **The owner**, and **the admins of a workspace**, can open and edit every
  agent in that workspace, personal ones included. An admin edits an agent in a
  workspace they do not administer only if it was shared with them as Can edit.
- **AptiveAI** can open anything by link and edit any agent.

<Aside>
A task you can only view reads "You can view this task". Its live view is
view-only: you see the screen, and your mouse and keyboard reach nothing.
</Aside>

## Continue in my own task

On a task you can only view, **Continue in my own task** starts a new task of
yours from that point. It keeps the conversation so far, with the same agent,
on your own computer and browser, and nothing you do there touches the
original. It needs an agent you can use; otherwise ask its owner to share it.

## Personal agents

An agent you own that is not shared with Everyone is **personal**. You can have
up to **10**; an agent shared with Everyone does not count. To make another,
delete one you no longer need or share one with the workspace.

## When someone leaves

When a person is removed from the enterprise, everything shared with them is
unshared. During a trial the automations they created are paused with the note
"Paused because the person who created it left the workspace", and an owner can
open them and turn them back on; on a subscription they are deleted when the
person's seat ends at the end of the billing period. Their agents and skills
stay, and owners and the admins of an agent's workspace can still edit their
agents.

## Links

**Copy link** in the share box (tasks and automations) gives a short address. It
does not grant access: the person who opens it still needs to be someone it is
shared with, or an owner.
